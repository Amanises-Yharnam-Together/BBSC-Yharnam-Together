- **Teams of five.** A team now takes five hunters, each with their own place on the team screen and the party bars.
- **Spectating follows your teammate** (Amanises's idea). While you are down and watch a teammate, your unseen body follows them, so their part of the map and the players around them stay in view. It never follows into a boss fight you were not in.
- **Team duels.** Fight your teammates: every hunter for themselves, or in teams. The last team standing wins.
- **Lobby.** Every hunter online shows with their team. Teammates no longer appear under Hunters online, and Invite only shows for a hunter who is in no team.
- **Quick match** on public servers, from the team screen's list: it finds an open team for you, or opens one others can join.
- **Accounts for the public server.** In Co-op setup, pick Global, then sign in or create an account with a name and a password. A recovery code (shown once, keep it) lets you set a new password if you forget it, and Change password and Sign out are there too. The password goes to the public server only, over HTTPS, and is never saved on your PC. The public server opens later: until then, play on a Local server as before.
- **Discord status** (optional, off by default): show your game on Discord. On a public server, friends can join you from Discord.
- **Every language.** Every text in the launcher is now in all 12 of its languages, the Co-op setup included.
- **Co-op setup:** the travel choice has its own step again (step 4 was empty), and the buttons under the game folder fit in every language.
- **Smoother co-op:** the co-op work each frame went from 2.3 ms to 0.4 ms, and Restart game keeps you online and keeps the frame pacing.
- **Public servers:** caps on bloodstain, message and ghost lists, and server limits set from load tests.
