**The public server is open**

- **Play on the public server, seamlesshunt.com.** Nobody has to start a server, open ports or share an IP address any more: pick Global in Co-op setup, sign in, and press PLAY. Hunters from everywhere, quick match, and your account on any PC.
- **The launcher offers it once.** When you start this version, it asks whether to make the public server your default. Switch to the public server opens Co-op setup on the sign-in; Keep Local leaves everything as it was. You can change it at any time in Co-op settings, and Local servers work as before.
- **Accounts.** Sign in or create an account with a name and a password. A recovery code (shown once, keep it) lets you set a new password if you forget it, and Change password and Sign out are there too. The password goes to the public server only, over HTTPS, and is never saved on your PC. The recovery code can now be selected, and Copy says Copied.
- **No server buttons on the public server.** Start server and Stop server show only while Local is chosen: on the public server there is nothing to start.
- **Team rules are the leader's, in the game.** On the team screen's settings page the leader sets Enemies (Auto or Fixed), Enemy strength, Rot, Travel together and Boss tempo, and every teammate plays by them. Enemies are Auto by default. On the public server the launcher has no Enemies or Travel steps; on a Local server they stay.
- **Fair play.** Release builds have no cheats, and the public server notes saves that were edited.

**Also new**

- **Phantom glow switch** (optional): show your teammates without the blue phantom glow, on your screen only.
- **Co-op settings, not the setup again.** After the first Done, the Co-op page opens as plain settings: pick any tab, Done on every tab.
- **New name and credits:** BBSC: Yharnam Together, with the supporters, more testers and fuller thanks.
- **Smaller logs:** release builds write only warnings. Set coop_trace=1 in bbport.ini for a bug report.

**From 1.3.2** (never released on its own)

- **Teams of five.** A team now takes five hunters, each with their own place on the team screen and the party bars.
- **Spectating follows your teammate** (Amanises's idea). While you are down and watch a teammate, your unseen body follows them, so their part of the map and the players around them stay in view. It never follows into a boss fight you were not in.
- **Team duels.** Fight your teammates: every hunter for themselves, or in teams. The last team standing wins.
- **Lobby.** Every hunter online shows with their team. Teammates no longer appear under Hunters online, and Invite only shows for a hunter who is in no team.
- **Quick match** on public servers, from the team screen's list: it finds an open team for you, or opens one others can join.
- **Discord status** (optional, off by default): show your game on Discord. On a public server, friends can join you from Discord.
- **Every language.** Every text in the launcher is now in all 12 of its languages, the Co-op setup included.
- **Co-op setup:** the travel choice has its own step again (step 4 was empty), and the buttons under the game folder fit in every language.
- **Smoother co-op:** the co-op work each frame went from 2.3 ms to 0.4 ms, and Restart game keeps you online and keeps the frame pacing.
