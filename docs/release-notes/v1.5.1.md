**Fixes from your reports**

Thank you to everyone who reported bugs on Discord.

- **Invisible teammate after joining.** A guest's game could stay "loading" after the join's reload, so the host never saw it and enemies ignored it. A guest who is standing in the world now counts as loaded.
- **A guest keeps its own progress.** In a friend's world a guest's own lamps and Dream headstones stay usable, including Old Yharnam and Hemwick. A new world made after visiting a friend no longer starts from the friend's progress.
- **GPU crash on a new character** (NVIDIA, "device lost"): fixed. Thanks, sousalucasc. The launcher's error log also no longer grows by megabytes.
- **Boss fights:** a death with a teammate still fighting beside you now spectates instead of wiping the party.
- **Shops:** armour a teammate buys no longer sells out in your shop.
- **Rom** no longer casts nonstop at a faster boss tempo.
- **The team screen says when your game isn't signed in to the game server**, instead of leaving you invisible to teammates.
- Fixed: a rare freeze when travelling together. Thanks, evill33t.
- Fixed: a game that rejoins after a link restart keeps its greeting.

**Crashes**

- From Supermedo's 1.9.8.5: fixes for the "render command list" and NPC talk crashes, more GPU heap, and the game ends at once when its window closes.
- GOTY copies that report version 01.00 now get the 1.09 fixes.

**Languages**

- **Thai:** the launcher and the in-game menus. Thanks, oat018. A few of the newest texts are still in English.
- Chinese (Simplified): a team name now draws with the game's own font.

**Launcher**

- A keyboard **Walk** key you can bind (no default key).
- Mods marked "Not loaded" explain why.
- The camera auto-rotation setting is easier to find.
- A note on how voices follow the game's language.

**Linux**

- A native Linux build of the port. Thanks, Tutuzaooo.

**Under the hood**

- A purple or red screen after travel is now logged with its cause, for the next fix.
