**New**

- **Joining is cleaner.** A hunter who joins a team reloads in place once and is then a full player in the host's world, not a phantom. It costs one short loading screen on joining and removes a family of guest-only bugs.
- **Party settings live in the game.** The team's rules are set on the team screen's settings page only, by the leader, and are kept for the leader's next team. The launcher no longer asks for them.
- **Custom enemy strength.** On the same page the leader can pick Custom and set the enemies' health, damage and poise by hand.
- **Friendly fire** (optional, off by default): a team rule that makes teammates' attacks hurt each other. With it on, an enemy targets only the player whose game runs it.

**Fixes from your reports**

Thank you to everyone who reported bugs on Discord. This version is the result of going through all of them.

- **Every copy of the game works in co-op.** Dumps of the US, Asian, Japanese and GOTY releases showed a flashing "test" HUD and a grey Create a team. Seamless co-op now runs on every Bloodborne release.
- **Saves no longer roll back.** A host who played alone after a team session could lose progress. Each save keeps its own world aside, a save loaded alone never takes the team's world as its own, and updates never touch saves.
- **Brazilian Portuguese and Latin American Spanish** work on any copy of the game, European ones included.
- **Smoother co-op on AMD cards.** The game no longer falls back to V-Sync on AMD, and late frames are shown at once. Choosing 30 FPS now stays 30 FPS in co-op.
- **Sign-in to the public server.** The launcher signs in through Windows' own certificates (fixes "no answer from the server" on some PCs), a busy game is no longer banned for flooding, and when the server refuses you the launcher and the game say why instead of "not signed in to PlayStation Network".
- **Nameplates** say Host over the team's host and Guest over the others.

**Enemies and bosses**

- Fixed: an enemy a guest had just killed could come back, respawning the area and giving free echoes.
- Fixed: enemies kept their old maximum health when the number of hunters on the map changed.
- Fixed: items a guest picked up came back after its game crashed and rejoined.
- Fixed: a hunter woken from spectating saw the boss's health bar before its own fight began (Rom).
- Fixed: fog walls at Rom, in the Dream and in chalice dungeons stayed up for guests.

**Chalice dungeons**

- A team wipe wakes everyone at the layer's lamp.
- Doors opened and layers beaten are shared by the team inside one dungeon.

**The Dream and items**

- Fixed: the Doll stuck kneeling after Circle or a teammate's hit, and the Small Hair Ornament now has its "Give".
- Fixed: key items a guest gained as a phantom were lost after closing the game.
- The Bath messengers' attire is each player's own.

**Launcher**

- The 3D map section sees a game folder picked in Co-op setup (thanks, CLS_AMG).
- Bandicam, FPS Monitor and the GOG overlay are kept out of the game, and when the game closes at start the launcher names the likely cause.
- Frame generation shows only with Experimental features on.
- Smaller fixes: the server stops when the launcher closes, Bloodborne.exe and Steam copies, GOTY DLC, a stray game folder entry.

**Under the hood**

- Emulator fixes from shadPS4 (to 11-09) and engine changes from Supermedo's 1.9.8.4: two freezes, faster file reads, and a crash guard for online events without data.
- Broken textures after travel: the cause is now logged for the next fix, at no cost in performance.
